Hides both players' in-game crosshairs for every weapon, for VR play with
VRLF, where you aim with the gun itself. Menu, calibration, name entry and
continue-screen pointers are left as they are.

Install
1. Close the game.
2. Back up your game's g6\aa\data\programs\perm folder.
3. Copy the g6 folder from this zip into your Aliens Armageddon game folder
   (the folder that holds "game", "g6" and "hasp") and overwrite when asked.

Undo
Put your backed-up perm folder back.
