Hides both players' in-game crosshairs for The Walking Dead (Raw Thrills,
2017) for VR play with VRLF, where you aim with the gun itself. Covers the
crossbow and every pickup weapon, the melee weapons and the shot flash.
Menu, calibration, game select and name entry pointers are left as they are.

Install
1. Close the game.
2. Back up your game's pm\g6\walkingdead\data\programs\perm folder.
3. Copy the g6 folder from this zip into your game's pm folder (the folder
   that holds "game" and "g6") and overwrite when asked.

Undo
Put your backed-up perm folder back.
